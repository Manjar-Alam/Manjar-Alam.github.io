
# Day 21. DELHI-THE URBAN FABRIC

## Overview

- **Theme:** OpenStreetMap
- **Project type:** Urban morphology and building footprint mapping
- **Study area:** Delhi, India

This project explores the urban fabric of Delhi through its building footprints obtained from OpenStreetMap. The map presents the spatial arrangement and distribution of buildings across the city, revealing patterns of urban development, built-up concentrations and variations in the city's physical structure.

By visualizing individual building footprints, the map offers a detailed perspective of Delhi's built environment, illustrating how spatial data can be used to understand the morphology and structure of a rapidly evolving metropolitan landscape.

## Key takeaways

- Individual building footprints reveal the spatial structure and morphological characteristics of Delhi's built environment.
- The distribution of buildings illustrates variations in urban form and development intensity across different parts of the city.
- OpenStreetMap provides a valuable open geospatial resource for large-scale urban mapping and visualization.
- The mapped building footprints represent available OpenStreetMap data and may not include every existing building in Delhi.
