
# Day 23. Taste: A Taste of Italy

## Overview

- **Theme:** Taste
- **Project type:** Mapping the Culinary Geography
- **Study area:** Italy

Italy's culinary identity is deeply connected to its geography, history, culture, and regional traditions. From northern Alpine specialties to southern Mediterranean cuisine, every region has its own distinctive food heritage.

This map explores Italy through a curated collection of 28 regional food specialties, represented by city-level origin or association points and mapped across the country's 21 NUTS-2 regions.

## Key takeaways

- The map presents the culinary diversity of Italy through 28 curated food specialties across 21 NUTS-2 regions.
- Thematic colours distinguish culinary categories, while regional shading represents the number of curated specialties assigned to each region.
- The mapped locations illustrate city-level culinary associations and do not necessarily represent official production locations or an exhaustive inventory of Italian food heritage.
