
# Day 22. One World-Six Perspectives
![Project overview image](/30_day_map/maps_png/day_22_projections.png)
## Overview

- **Theme:** Projections
- **Project type:** Comparative cartography and world map projections
- **Study area:** Global

This project explores how different cartographic projections represent the same Earth in different ways. Although the geographic data remains unchanged, each projection transforms the three-dimensional surface of the Earth into a two-dimensional map, resulting in variations in shape, area, distance and spatial appearance.

Using global country boundaries from Natural Earth, the map presents six different projections side by side, demonstrating how projection choices influence the visual representation and interpretation of our world.

## Key takeaways

- Every map projection introduces some form of distortion when representing the curved Earth on a flat surface.
- Equal-area projections preserve relative area, while conformal projections preserve local angles and shapes.
- Compromise projections balance different types of distortion rather than eliminating them.
- The choice of projection should depend on the geographical extent, analytical purpose and intended map application.
