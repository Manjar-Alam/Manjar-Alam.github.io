
# 19. The Emirates in the Air

## Overview

- **Theme:** Smell
- **Project type:** Thematic GIS mapping and sensory geography
- **Study area:** Ajman, Sharjah and Umm al-Quwain, United Arab Emirates

This project explores the sensory geography of the Northern Emirates by mapping places associated with distinctive everyday smells. Using OpenStreetMap points of interest (POIs), locations were classified into five thematic categories: Food & Spices, Nature & Flowers, Perfumes & Incense, Sea & Coast, and Urban & Industry.

The map uses a light, warm cartographic style inspired by the colours and atmosphere of the UAE. Distinctive colours represent different smell-associated categories, creating a visual interpretation of the region's sensory landscape. Rather than mapping directly measured odours, the project uses mapped places as spatial proxies to imagine where different smells may be encountered.

## Key takeaways

- Food & Spices emerged as the dominant mapped category, reflecting the large number of food-related POIs in the selected emirates.
- Nature & Flowers, Perfumes & Incense, and Urban & Industry add contrasting dimensions to the sensory geography of the region.
- The map offers a creative spatial interpretation of smell by connecting everyday places with their potential sensory associations.
- The mapped locations are OpenStreetMap-derived proxies, not direct odour measurements or a comprehensive record of actual smell intensity.
- The absence of mapped locations in the Sea & Coast category reflects the current classification and available POI data, rather than the absence of coastal smells.
