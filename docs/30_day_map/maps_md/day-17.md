
# Day 17.  Light & Dark
![Project overview image](/30_day_map/maps_png/day_17_light_dark.png)
## Overview

- **Theme:** Light & Dark
- **Project type:** ANTARCTICA: BETWEEN LIGHT & DARK
- **Study area:** Antarctica

This project explores the dramatic interplay of light and darkness across Antarctica, a continent defined by extreme seasonal variations in daylight. From the continuous sunlight of the austral summer to the prolonged darkness of the austral winter, the map presents Antarctica's distinctive polar environment through contrasting cartographic tones.

Using physical geographic features and bathymetric data, the map highlights the Antarctic landmass, surrounding Southern Ocean, and underwater topography. A contrasting visual palette of light and dark shades creates a striking representation of this remote polar landscape.

## Key takeaways

- The map illustrates Antarctica's distinctive polar geography and its surrounding ocean environment.
- Contrasting light and dark cartographic elements create visual depth and emphasize the frozen landscape.
- Bathymetric data adds a deeper geographical perspective by revealing the underwater topography surrounding Antarctica.
- The concept draws inspiration from the extreme seasonal daylight cycle, where continuous daylight and prolonged darkness define the Antarctic environment.
