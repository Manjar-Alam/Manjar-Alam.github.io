
# Day 2.  The Ganga: A River and Its Tributaries

## Overview

- **Theme:** Lines
- **Project type:** River network and hydrological mapping
- **Study area:** Ganga River Basin, India

This project explores the Ganga River and its tributaries through a linear representation of the river network. Using HydroRIVERS data, the map highlights the interconnected drainage system, illustrating the Ganga's extensive network of tributaries and waterways across the basin. The cartographic design emphasizes river connectivity and the branching patterns that shape one of India's most significant river systems.
