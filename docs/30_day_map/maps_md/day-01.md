
# Day 1.  A Spatial Portrait of Delhi

## Overview

- **Theme:** Points
- **Project type:** Urban GIS and point-of-interest mapping
- **Study area:** Delhi, India

This project explores Delhi through its mapped points of interest, illustrating how location-based data can represent the city's public services, amenities, and urban destinations. The map uses a dark cartographic background with selective colours to distinguish POI categories and improve visual contrast.

## Key takeaways

- The map provides a spatial overview of mapped points of interest across Delhi.
- Thematic colours and a restrained background help distinguish mapped locations.
- The mapped POIs represent available OpenStreetMap data, not necessarily every existing facility in Delhi.
