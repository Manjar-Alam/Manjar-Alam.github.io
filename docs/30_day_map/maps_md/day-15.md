
# Day 15. Chilika Lake: Beneath the Surface

## Overview

- **Theme:** Inside Out
- **Project type:** Bathymetric mapping and underwater landscape visualization
- **Study area:** Chilika Lake, Odisha, India

This project explores Chilika Lake from an inside-out perspective, revealing its hidden underwater topography through bathymetric visualization. Using the GLOBathy dataset, the map represents variations in lake depth and provides a glimpse into the submerged landscape beneath the water surface. A graduated colour palette highlights depth variations, transforming the familiar lake into an unseen geographical landscape.

## Key takeaways

- The map reveals the hidden underwater topography of Chilika Lake through bathymetric visualization.
- Graduated colours communicate spatial variations in estimated lake depth.
- The project demonstrates how geospatial datasets can expose geographical features that remain invisible from the surface.
- The GLOBathy dataset provides estimated bathymetric information and should not be interpreted as a direct, field-surveyed depth measurement.
