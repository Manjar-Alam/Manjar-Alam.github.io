
# Day 12. Power: THE WORLD POWERED-Who Consumes the Most Electricity?
![Project overview image](/30_day_map/maps_png/day_12_power.png)
## Overview

- **Theme:** Power
- **Project type:** Global Electricity Consumption per Capita, 2023
- **Study area:** World

This map explores the global distribution of electricity consumption per capita in 2023. A five-class choropleth map illustrates differences in electricity use across countries, highlighting regions with relatively low and high per-capita consumption.

The map also identifies the Top 5 Countries by Electricity Consumption per Capita, providing a comparative perspective on global electricity-use patterns.

**Data Sources**

## Key takeaways

- Electricity consumption per capita varies considerably across the world, reflecting differences in energy infrastructure, economic activity, industrial development and access to electricity.
- Global electricity consumption patterns across 258 countries and territories.

- Five categories representing very low to very high electricity consumption.

- Identification of the five highest-ranking countries by per-capita electricity use.

- Comparison of geographical differences in electricity consumption.
- Note: Electricity consumption per capita is a national-level indicator and should not be interpreted as household electricity consumption.
