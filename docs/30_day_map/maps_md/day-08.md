
# Day 8. UTOPIA: The Garden City
![Project overview image](/30_day_map/maps_png/day_8_utopia.png)

## Overview

- **Theme:** Utopia
- **Project type:** Urban green-space mapping and sustainable landscape visualization
- **Study area:** Singapore

This project explores the concept of a greener urban future through a thematic map of Singapore, highlighting its green spaces, protected natural areas, water bodies, wetlands and waterways. Using OpenStreetMap data, the map illustrates the relationship between natural landscapes and the urban environment. It presents a vision of Singapore as a connected Garden City, where green and blue spaces contribute to a more sustainable urban landscape.

## Data & Tools
**Data Sources**

- [Geofabrik](https://www.geofabrik.de/)
- [DIVA-GIS](https://diva-gis.org/data.html)

**Tools Used**

* QGIS, ArcGIS Pro
* Python (GeoPandas, Pandas, Matplotlib, Folium) [Python Notebook](#Link)

## Key takeaways

- The map highlights the distribution of parks, forests, recreational spaces and protected natural areas across Singapore.
- Water bodies, wetlands and waterways illustrate the blue-green elements of the urban landscape.
- Thematic colours and a carefully designed cartographic layout distinguish natural areas from the surrounding urban environment.
- The map presents a conceptual vision of a greener and more connected Singapore, inspired by the Garden City approach.
