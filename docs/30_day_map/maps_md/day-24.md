
# Day 24. Lines That Connect India

## Overview

- **Theme:** Network
- **Project type:** Railway network mapping and transportation geography
- **Study area:** India

This project explores India's railway network through the spatial distribution and connectivity of railway lines across the country. The map illustrates how an extensive network of interconnected routes links major cities, towns, and regions, highlighting the geographical reach of India's railway infrastructure.
