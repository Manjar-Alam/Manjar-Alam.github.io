
# Day 11. The Sound of Delhi
![Project overview image](/30_day_map/maps_png/day_11_sound.png)
## Overview

- **Theme:** Sound
- **Project type:** Mapping the City's Urban Soundscape
- **Study area:** Delhi, India

This project explores the urban soundscape of Delhi by mapping the spatial distribution of potential sound-generating activities and contrasting urban environments. Using OpenStreetMap data, the map highlights major transportation corridors, railway networks, commercial centres, public activity locations, and green spaces. Illustrative concentric sound-wave rings highlight selected locations, including Connaught Place, Chandni Chowk, Kashmere Gate, Anand Vihar, IGI Airport, and Lodhi Garden, representing the diverse acoustic character of the city.

## Key takeaways

- The map illustrates how transportation networks, commercial centres, and public activity contribute to the diverse sound environments of Delhi.
- Major roads, railway networks, and transport hubs highlight potential sources of urban noise, while parks and green spaces represent potentially quieter environments.
- Concentric sound-wave symbols provide a visual representation of selected urban activity centres.
- The map demonstrates how GIS and OpenStreetMap data can be used to explore the relationship between urban activities and the city's soundscape.
- Note: This is a qualitative representation of potential sound environments based on mapped urban features, not a measured noise-level or decibel assessment.
