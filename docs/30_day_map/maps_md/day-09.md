
# Day 9.  Urban to Rural: India After Dark

## Overview

- **Theme:** Urban-Rural
- **Project type:** Nighttime light mapping and urban–rural spatial analysis
- **Study area:** India

This project explores the contrast between urban and rural landscapes across India using nighttime satellite observations. VIIRS nighttime light data for 2025 illustrates the spatial distribution and intensity of artificial lighting, highlighting major urban centres and relatively darker rural regions. A speculative visualization for 2125 further imagines how India's nighttime landscape could transform over the next century.

## Key takeaways

- VIIRS nighttime lights reveal the spatial contrast between illuminated urban centres and comparatively darker rural regions.
- The 2025 map provides an observation-based representation of India's nighttime lighting patterns.
- The speculative 2125 visualization illustrates a possible future nighttime landscape and is not a scientific prediction.
- Nighttime illumination offers a useful spatial indicator of human activity and urban development, although it does not directly represent population or development levels.
