## Overview

Developed an interactive crop monitoring field survey using ArcGIS Survey123 to facilitate structured collection of agricultural field observations. The application enables field-level data collection, geospatial referencing, and systematic recording of crop conditions and related agricultural information. The collected survey data supports crop monitoring, field assessment, and spatial analysis through the ArcGIS Online platform.

**Study Area:** Agricultural fields in India

**Duration:** Personal / Professional Project (2025–2026)

**Role:** Survey Design and GIS Data Management

**Status:** Completed

---

## Methods & Tools

### Data Collection

- Georeferenced field data collection using ArcGIS Survey123.
- Structured questionnaire design for agricultural field observations.
- Collection of crop-related information and field attributes.
- Location-based survey submissions for spatial identification of agricultural fields.
- Organization and management of submitted survey records.

### Tools Used

- ArcGIS Survey123
- ArcGIS Online
- ArcGIS Web Maps
- ArcGIS Pro

---

## Key Features & Outcomes

- Developed a digital field survey for systematic crop monitoring.
- Enabled geospatially referenced agricultural data collection.
- Standardized field observations through structured survey forms.
- Facilitated centralized storage and visualization of survey submissions.
- Supported efficient agricultural field assessment and GIS-based data management.
